Print & Pattern

For this project, I looked into the brand Fugazi and developed a set of prints that would be suited to a future S/S26 line. I took their themes of irony and irreverence to build these prints. The "Bent Plaid" print is a classic plaid but distorted to be wavy. The "Double Duck Camo" pattern is a stacked duck canvas pattern and the shape of the blobs is derived from print #3. The "Scenic Blaze" pattern is inspired by vintage sleeping bags and british sceinc prints.
